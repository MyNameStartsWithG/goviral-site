Privacy policy

GoViral · Last updated 8 November 2026

GoViral is a personal tool run by NameIsGuven (contact: benimadimguven@gmail.com). It publishes clips of my own livestreams to my own social media accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Whose data it uses

Only mine. GoViral signs in to accounts I own: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Kick and Twitch. It does not sign in to or collect data from anyone else's account.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: uploading videos to my channel, and reading my channel's basic details and video statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook: publishing posts and reels to my account, and reading their statistics and comments.
  • TikTok: uploading videos to my account.
  • Kick and Twitch: knowing when my stream starts and ends, and reading chat messages during my streams. Chat is used only to measure how active the chat is at each moment, so the tool can find highlights.

Viewers' chat messages

Messages viewers write in my stream chat are public on Kick and Twitch. GoViral stores them on my computer only to find the busiest moments of a stream, and deletes them within 30 days. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Where data is kept

Recordings, transcripts and access tokens stay on my own computer. Tokens are never published. The only data sent out is the clips I approve, which go to my own accounts.

Google user data

GoViral's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from YouTube is used only to upload my clips and to show me how they perform.

Removing access

I can revoke GoViral's access at any time from each platform's security settings, for example at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google.
